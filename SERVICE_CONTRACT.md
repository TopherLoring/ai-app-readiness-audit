# AI App Readiness Audit — draft contract

Not approved for sale or customer data. Prices are unvalidated. Target: owners preparing a small web app for launch. Proposed basic $250–$350: one authorized test environment, up to five customer-specified routes and two journeys at desktop and mobile sizes; use test data only with permission. Expanded $500–$750: separately scoped assessment only after browser capability and economics are verified; do not advertise now. Limits need mock validation.

Customer provides allowed origins, journeys, authorization and approved test account or customer-assisted access; never request plaintext passwords. Deliver HTML/PDF and JSON with reproducible steps, safe screenshots, severity, confidence and prioritized checklist. No penetration test, certification, comprehensive accessibility or compliance assessment, exhaustive device coverage, fixes or guarantee of a bug-free application. Retests and repairs are separately quoted. Acceptance: agreed journeys and artifacts, not a guarantee of readiness.

## Proposed terms for owner and customer review
Prepaid invoice using an existing provider; never collect payment credentials. Begin only after payment and written authorization. Target delivery three business days after complete intake, subject to scope confirmation; unmeasured. One factual or clarity correction round requested within five business days of delivery; no new analysis, implementation or retest included. Acceptance on written confirmation or after five business days without a reported material nonconformity, subject to signed agreement. Before work begins, cancellation refund less disclosed nonrefundable processing fees; once work begins, discuss proportional refund for undelivered work. Correct material nonconformities or agree on a refund. These are draft terms requiring owner review and customer agreement; they are not legal advice. Delete data per agreed retention schedule.
